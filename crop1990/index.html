Crop and Grassland Maps of Switzerland

Annual maps of 51 crop, grassland and land cover classes for all of Switzerland from 1985 to 2025. The maps are based on Landsat (30 m) up to 2017 and on Sentinel-2 (10 m) from 2018.

How to use

  • Move the timeline or press play to step through the years.
  • Click the map to see the class history of a pixel over all years.
  • Click classes or group names in the legend to show only those, and to see their mapped area over time. Collapse a group to show all its classes in one colour.
  • Confidence shows the calibrated model probability of the shown class.
  • The current view can be shared by copying the link.
